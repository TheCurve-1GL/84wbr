The building

A Victorian building, given a new layout.

Ambrad Group converted this Victorian mixed-use building on Wandsworth Bridge Road into four self-contained flats.

The conversion included a loft conversion, a rear dormer, a second-floor extension and a side-return extension. The building sits in the Studdridge Street Conservation Area, and full planning permission was granted in June 2023.

The result is a mix of homes under one roof: one three-bedroom flat, two two-bedroom flats and a one-bedroom flat.

  • Address: 84 Wandsworth Bridge Road, Fulham, London.
  • Original building: Victorian, mixed-use.
  • Conservation area: Studdridge Street.
